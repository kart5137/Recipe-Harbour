# Model card: Recipe Harbour produce classifier

> Generated by `model/model_card.py` from run `v2` on 2026-11-22. Numbers come from the run's `metrics.json`; do not edit by hand.

## What it does

Recognises **one fruit or vegetable per photo** and suggests the top 3 ingredients with confidence. It runs in the browser (TensorFlow.js); photos never leave the device. It is a convenience for adding ingredients, not a food-safety tool.

## Model

- MobileNetV2 (ImageNet weights, 224×224 input) with a softmax head over 24 classes; the top 55 backbone layers fine-tuned.
- Exported to TensorFlow.js with 16-bit weights (float16); the exported model gives the same top answer as the trained Keras model on 100 % of 200 real test photos. 8-bit quantisation was tried and rejected (it changed most answers).
- Size: about 4.7 MB.

## How well it works (real photos it never saw)

**Overall:** top-1 74 %, top-3 90 % on 2,205 real-photo crops (macro top-1 across classes 71 %).

Runs compared (same test set):

|---|---|---|---|
| real_photos_only | 40,128 | 70 % | 89 % |
| real_plus_fruits360 | 50,430 | 71 % | 89 % |
| finetuned (chosen) | 25,215 | 74 % | 90 % |

Training examples: the head-only runs count each training image twice (the original and one augmented copy); fine-tuning uses each image once per pass, with fresh random augmentation. `real_photos_only` leaves out the Fruits-360 lab photos.

## Known weaknesses

Most frequent mix-ups on the test set:

- zucchini taken for cucumber: 55 times
- cucumber taken for zucchini: 30 times
- tomato taken for strawberry: 27 times
- tomato taken for radish: 11 times
- zucchini taken for tomato: 11 times
- apple taken for peach: 10 times
- apple taken for pomegranate: 10 times
- cucumber taken for tomato: 9 times

- Zucchini and cucumber look alike in photos; the right one is usually in the top 3 but not first.
- Apple, pear, mango and fig are not reliable enough to offer.
- Not trained for: several items in one photo, cut or cooked food, packaged food (use the barcode scanner), onions, garlic, lemons and other produce without real-photo test data.
